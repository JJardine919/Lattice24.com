Region is almost always the largest single lever, and it is usually the one nobody models. Moving the same fleet between grids can change the reported figure several-fold without touching a single workload.

What this does not claim. This models your current footprint. It does not claim a reduction, because we have not measured one on your fleet. Anyone quoting you a percentage saving before seeing your data is guessing.

The useful question after this one is where reduction spend buys the most tonnes — scheduling, placement, utilisation, and which workloads justify their emissions. That is optimisation work on your real numbers, and it starts with a footprint you can defend.

Method and sources

Annual electricity = GPU count × TDP × utilisation × hours/day × 365 × PUE.
Annual emissions = electricity × regional grid carbon intensity.

Grid intensity: Ember, Global Electricity Review 2025 (2024 data) — global 445, US 384, EU 213, China 560, India 708, Russia 449 gCO₂/kWh; UK 177, Germany 330, France 41 gCO₂e/kWh from Ember yearly electricity data.
GPU TDP: manufacturer specifications.
PUE: your own figure if you have one. 1.2 is typical of modern hyperscale facilities; older enterprise rooms run considerably higher.
Carbon price: Alberta TIER fund price frozen at CAD $95/tonne since May 2025. TIER credits trade well below that — under $20 — so both are shown. TIER prices only emissions above a facility benchmark, so this is exposure per tonne abated, not a tax bill.
